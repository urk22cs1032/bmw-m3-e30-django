ABOUT BMW M3 E30

A LEGEND IN MOTORSPORT HISTORY

Born from motorsport, built for the road

The BMW M3 E30 represents one of the most iconic chapters in BMW Motorsport history. Born from motorsport and developed with performance at its core, it became a symbol of BMW's performance heritage.

The E30 M3 was developed for competition and road-going performance, combining a lightweight chassis with the high-revving S14 four-cylinder engine and motorsport-focused engineering.

This project brings that heritage into a modern digital showroom, providing a clean way to explore and manage BMW vehicle records.
